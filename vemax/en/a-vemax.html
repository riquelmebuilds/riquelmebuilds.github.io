About Vemax

Since 1999, turning drawings into parts to aerospace standards.

Based in Botucatu since 1999, Vemax is one of the fastest-growing manufacturers in inland São Paulo.

We have a highly qualified elite team and are certified to ISO 9001:2015 and AS9100D:2016.

We bring the excellence of aerospace culture to everything we do.

Our history

  1. 1999

    The beginning, as MCA

    Founded by Mr. Mauro Costa de Abreu with three employees, providing machining services to NEIVA.

  2. 2006

    ISO 9001 and AS9100 certification

    An effective Quality Management System brings the company up to aerospace industry standards.

  3. 2009

    New management

    New management takes over and, together with the team, starts an expansion plan.

  4. 2011

    Vemax is born

    MCA (the founder’s initials) becomes VEMAX, a name that reflects what is expected of the company.

  5. 2017

    First Q Day and FNQ assessment

    Assisted self-assessment by FNQ based on the MEG excellence model, and the first Q Day.

  6. 2018

    PEEV

    Launch of the Vemax Operational Excellence Program, based on the MEG model.

Q Day

Q Day refers to World Quality Day, established by the UN in 1990 and celebrated on the second Thursday of November.

Its goal is to highlight the importance of quality for productivity and competitiveness. We held our first Q Day in 2017 and have repeated it every year since.
